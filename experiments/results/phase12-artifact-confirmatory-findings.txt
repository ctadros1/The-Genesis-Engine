No threshold was changed after the data was seen. The plan's risk table
names C12.3 as the criterion most likely to return null and the
pre-registration (section 3) said the same of C12.1 in advance. What the
pre-registration could not say in advance, and the census now does, is
*why*: not because no lineage could reach the actions - every world's
living population bound every one of the five - but because the actions,
once bound, fired into a world that almost never satisfied their
preconditions, and nothing in 60,000 ticks selected them into doing so.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE NUMBER THAT EXPLAINS EVERYTHING ELSE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Under A, over 30 worlds and 3,773,908,974 organism-ticks, pick_up, place
and combine were **requested 48,137,380 times and succeeded 41,703 times**:
one success per 1,154 requests, 11 ppm of organism-ticks succeeded against
12,755 ppm fired. Per world the success rate ran 1-63 ppm (median 5); the
fire rate 7,353-30,080 ppm (median 11,057).

The refusals say what stood in the way (A, all 30 worlds):

  NothingHeld      45,056,105   place, combine or drop requested by an
                                organism holding nothing
  NoTarget         14,115,304   pick_up requested with no free object in
                                reach (2 m)
  HeldCap           4,095,691   pick_up requested while already holding
                                the one object the cap allows
  Depleted          2,960,362   strike on a cell already stripped of yield
  CapacityExceeded     29,568   the nearest object was too heavy
  DepthCap              1,255   (combine, at cap 4)
  Contested             1,064
  OccupancyCap            331
  JointFailed              86   (25% of attempts, the floor as configured)

So the shape is: organisms that bound the channels requested the actions
on roughly one tick in a hundred, all the time, whether or not there was
anything to act on - and there almost never was. Objects existed
(1,157 live per world at the horizon, 7% of the 16,384 cells, most of
them fragments and carcasses) but were rarely within one cell of an
organism at the moment it asked; and the two-step sequences (hold, then
place; hold, then combine) fired their second step overwhelmingly while
holding nothing. 40,139 pick-ups succeeded; of what was picked up, 21,918
objects were dropped, 10,170 fell at the holder's death, 1,301 were placed
and 263 were combined.

That is a different null from Phase 11's (D-099). There the conjunction
was unreachable by mutation. Here it was reached in every world (section
5) and the actions ran; the coupling between *perceiving* an object
(cues 17-22 were offered) and *requesting* the right action at the right
moment did not evolve at this rate and horizon.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. C12.1 - ACTIONS USED, NOT JUST FIRED. UNMET, 0 OF 30.
----------------------------------------------------------------

Decision rule, from the pre-registration: successful pick_up + place +
combine per million organism-ticks, A minus C on the same seed, at least
+10 ppm, in at least 20 of 30 worlds. Under C every requested action is
charged and counted, so C's rate is the firing rate and A's is the success
rate: the criterion asks whether making the actions *work* makes lineages
succeed more often than they would merely fire.

  A success rate  median 5 ppm     (1 .. 63)
  C fire rate     median 11,548 ppm (5,515 .. 29,475)
  A - C           0 of 30 reaching +10 ppm; 0 of 30 positive at all;
                  mean -12,819 ppm, median -11,675, CI [-14,695, -11,251],
                  p(SESOI) = 1.000

Supplementary, as pre-registered: the *fire* rate under A against C
(successes plus refusals of the same three actions, from the log):
16 of 30 positive, mean -449 ppm, median +304, CI [-2,466, +1,455],
p = 0.427. Lineages under A fired the actions neither more nor less than
under C. Whatever the actions did in A - and they extracted 640,209
objects, fractured 1,341,287 and fed 923 million milli of carcass energy
back to the population (section 6) - it did not change how often the
lineages asked for them within 60,000 ticks.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. C12.2 - STRUCTURES PERSIST AND MATTER. UNMET.
----------------------------------------------------------------

(a) Median placed-object episode longer than the median organism lifespan,
in 15 of 30. **7 of 30.** Under A there were 1,301 placed episodes across
30 worlds (median 7 per world, range 0-691; 24 worlds had at least one);
the median episode lasted 110 ticks against a median organism lifespan of
985. The seven worlds that met the clause are the ones with very few
placements that stayed put (seed 12013: three episodes, median 16,694
ticks; 12020: one episode, 15,752 ticks; 12017: 42 episodes, 3,135) - a
placed stone in an empty cell nobody visits does persist. It is
persistence without frequency. Under B (ephemeral) every episode closes in
its own tick: 26 worlds have episodes and every median lifetime is 0,
which is the control behaving as designed.

(b) Stratified exposure effect on reproductive output positive, with at
least 20 exposed organisms, in 20 of 30. **11 of 30 positive; 22 of 30
had 20 or more exposed.** 9,773 organisms across A spent at least 5% of
their life on a cell holding a live placed object (median 131 per world,
range 0-4,452); the per-world effect (exposed minus unexposed offspring per
thousand ticks of life, weighted over birth bands, milli) ran -688 to +143
with a median of -35.5: exposed organisms had, if anything, slightly
*fewer* offspring per tick of life. With 1,301 placements in 3.77 billion
organism-ticks the exposure is thin and the estimate is what a thin
exposure gives; nothing here suggests the placed objects mattered to
anyone.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. C12.3 - CUMULATIVE DEPENDENCY. UNMET, 5 OF 30.
----------------------------------------------------------------

Decision rule: live composites of depth two or more per thousand living
organisms, sampled every 1,000 ticks, last third minus first third of the
run at least +0.5 per thousand, in N = 20 of 30 (fixed in the
pre-registration). **5 of 30**: seeds 12018 (33 depth-2 composites over
the run, last-third mean 4.78 per thousand), 12021 (38; 1.85), 12022 (6;
0.98), 12025 (4; 0.92), 12029 (18; 0.68). Every world's first third is
zero. 15 of 30 worlds ever made a depth-2 composite; 60 were made in all,
from 263 combinations, and 131 composites were struck back apart. Under D
(depth cap 0) the count is zero in every world, as constructed, so the
five are the depth cap being open and not an artefact of the analysis.

That is a real, small, positive: elaboration to depth two *happened* by
chance in half the worlds and rose over the run in five, from a base of
zero. It is not the frequency the criterion asked for.

----------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE REACHABILITY CENSUS: THIS IS NOT A REACHABILITY NULL
----------------------------------------------------------------

Owed beside every null (pre-registration section 6). Read from each
world's final snapshot: which of the five action channels the living
genomes bind, and the two conjunctions a placement or a combination needs
in one line of descent.

  Condition A, 30 worlds, 91,987 living organisms at the horizon:
    worlds with >= 1 living genome bound to
      pick_up 30   drop 30   place 30   strike 30   combine 30
      pick_up AND place 28   pick_up AND combine 28
    living organisms binding at least one action: 22,287 (24%)
    per world (median): pick_up 176, place 136, combine 112, strike 115,
      pick_up-and-place 6, pick_up-and-combine 5.5
    bind operations applied over the run: 603,412 (median 20,908 per world)
  Conditions B, C, D: the same to within a few percent (they share the
  seeds and the supply), which is the common-mode design working.

So the D-114 operator did what it was added for: at 0.25 per birth over
2.44 million births per arm, every action was reachable and reached in
every world, and the two-channel conjunctions in 28 of 30. Phase 11's null
was "no lineage could reach the action"; this one is "every lineage could,
many did, and firing it did not pay enough to be shaped in 60,000 ticks".
The next campaign's knobs are the ones the pre-registration named - the
costs and the rate - and one it did not: the reach (2 m, one cell), which
is what NoTarget's 14 million refusals are about.

----------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THE OBJECTS DID TO THE ECOLOGY, AND A CONFOUND IN THE C ARM
----------------------------------------------------------------

The artifact half changed the ecology in one way the plan did not name:
carcasses are objects, and objects with energy are eaten. Under A the
population took **923 million milli of energy back from carcass objects**
in 23.4 million consumption events (0.24 milli per organism-tick), of
2,966 million milli of carcass energy created; the rest decayed away
(2,044 million) or went to dust. Under D and B the same to within 2%.

**Under C, none of it: the inert path returns before consumption runs, so
carcass objects exist (293,560 created) but are never eaten, and every
milli of their energy decays (1,953 of 1,960 million).** That is a
difference between C and A that the pre-registration did not state - it
said "carcass objects still exist under C (they are ecology, not action)"
and they do, but their energy is unrecoverable there. The demography
shows it: starvation deaths 2,026,386 under C against 1,911,554 under A
(+6%), deaths by damage 206,738 against 354,716 (-42%; poorer organisms
contest less), births 2,407,034 against 2,440,031, final population
93,005 against 91,987. It does not touch the verdicts - C12.2 and C12.3
do not read C, and C12.1's 0 of 30 is decided by a factor of a thousand
between successes and fires that no ecological difference could close -
but it means the "actions fire but confer no effect" arm also had less
food, and a future C arm must run consumption and decay and skip only the
five verbs. That is a kernel change under a new policy version, recorded
as an open question rather than made after the fact (D-118).

Other ecology under A: 640,209 extractions stripped cells (2,960,362
strikes hit an already-depleted cell), 1,537,351 strikes hit objects and
1,341,287 fractured them into 861,243 fragments and dust; 568,060 objects
decayed away; the ledger balanced to the milli in every world at every
invariant check, and no world reached the object cap.

----------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT WAS FOUND ON THE WAY
----------------------------------------------------------------

- The event-log decoder refused every log in which a bind operation had
  ever been rejected: its operator range stopped at OP_TRANSPOSITION (5)
  and OP_BINDING is 6. The very first world's log tripped it. Fixed on
  the reader side (the logs are correct), with the round-trip sample
  extended to carry a rejected bind; the CLI test's toy campaign had not
  produced one in 3,000 ticks, which is why it passed.
- Under C every one of the five counters advances by fires (that is the
  design), so `artifact_picked_up` in the manifest means "picked up" in
  A, B, D and "asked to pick up" in C. The report's `successes` column
  carries the same meaning by construction, as the pre-registration says.

----------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHAT THE RESULT LICENSES, IN THE REVIEW'S VOCABULARY
----------------------------------------------------------------

Nothing above review 10.2 Level 0. Interaction happened (extraction,
fracture, carrying, placement, combination all occurred, at low rates);
controlled transport (Level 1) is not shown, since successful pick-ups
were 0.2% of pick-up requests (40,139 of about 18.3 million) and their
timing was not distinguishable from firing at baseline. Persistence was shown for a handful of objects
without a matched fitness signal; elaboration to depth two occurred at a
frequency the criterion did not accept. The phase's value, as the plan
said it would be, is a measured negative with controls and a mechanism
census that says where the negative lives.
