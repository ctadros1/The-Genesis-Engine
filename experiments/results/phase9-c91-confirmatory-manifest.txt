| # Phase 9 confirmatory campaign: C9.1, C9.2, and C9.5.
| #
| # ============================================================
| # PRE-REGISTRATION. Committed before the campaign was run.
| # ============================================================
| #
| # ECOLOGY, chosen by the rule stated in `phase9-ecology-sweep.campaign`
| # before that sweep ran: highest median generation count subject to no
| # extinctions across eight seeds and a final population of at least 200.
| # The sweep selected `k240` unambiguously - 61 median generations, 2,309
| # median population, minimum population 1,616, zero extinctions - against
| # k030 (5 of 8 extinct), k120 (1 of 8 extinct), m000 (58 generations) and
| # m040 (1 of 8 extinct). D-073 is why this had to be settled first:
| # structural evolution is bought with generations, and a campaign run in a
| # birth-limited world measures its birth rate instead.
| #
| # CLIMATE OFF, measured rather than assumed. `phase9-climate-probe.campaign`
| # ran climate on against climate off, seed-matched at 256x256 on eight seeds
| # that generate a full biome set. The structural signal did not move - median
| # mean-node count 3.073 dry against 3.095 with climate, median node count 3
| # and 9 distinct structures under both - while climate cost about 10 percent
| # of the generations (64 to 58) and needs the four-times-larger map. A
| # seasonal environment was worth testing because a stationary world might
| # simply not reward a larger network, which would make a structural null a
| # fact about the environment; it does not appear to be the binding factor.
| #
| # SEEDS 3001..3030, disjoint from the ecology sweep (1..8) and the climate
| # probe (2002..2011), so no seed that informed the design also carries the
| # result.
| #
| # TRANSPOSITION IS 0 IN EVERY CONDITION. With single-chromosome founders the
| # operator is provably inert (D-074): position within a chromosome is
| # determined by homology order, so there is nothing for it to do, and every
| # attempt is refused as `Inapplicable`. Leaving it enabled would add a
| # rejection stream that means nothing and would inflate the matched-load
| # arithmetic below with firings that change no genome.
| #
| # THE SWEPT FACTOR is the per-birth duplication rate, at 1x, 3x, and 10x its
| # documented default, with the deletion rate held at its default throughout.
| # What that actually varies is the duplication-to-deletion balance - 1:1,
| # 3:1, 10:1 - and that is the honest description, because at equal rates
| # genome size is at mutation-deletion equilibrium and has no expected growth
| # at all. ADR-0018's naming test: "a per-birth duplication rate" is a
| # physics constant of the model, like a mortality rate, not an outcome.
| #
| # EVERY RATE IS REPORTED AND THE CRITERION IS EVALUATED AT EACH. There is no
| # designated "primary" rate whose result is the headline and whose siblings
| # are supporting detail. The claim C9.1 can support is "structural evolution
| # occurs at duplication rate X and does not at rate Y", with the whole curve
| # published, which is both more informative than a single pass/fail and
| # impossible to select within after the fact.
| #
| # DECISION RULES, fixed here before the run:
| #
| #   C9.1a  median shift. Under an A condition, median expressed node count
| #          >= 4 OR median edge count >= 3 - that is, founder + 1, the
| #          smallest shift a median of integers can express - in at least
| #          20 of 30 seeds. Either quantity, not both: duplication grows node
| #          count and insertion grows edge count, so requiring both would make
| #          the criterion a test of which operator was enabled.
| #   C9.1b  diversity. Under an A condition, more than one distinct
| #          (node count, edge count) pair among the living, in at least
| #          25 of 30 seeds.
| #   C9.1c  control. Under B, structure invariant - median 3 nodes and
| #          2 edges, mean exactly 3000 and 2000 milli, at most one distinct
| #          structure - in 30 of 30 seeds. This is the instrument check: if
| #          the control drifts, the gating is broken and no A result means
| #          anything. Note it is checked on the mean as well as the median,
| #          because standing variation that has not spread would otherwise
| #          read as a frozen control.
| #   C9.2   stability. Under an A condition against B, seed-paired: median
| #          population within 25 percent of B **or better**, and median
| #          completed lifespan within 25 percent **or better**, each in at
| #          least 20 of 30 seeds. "Or better" is the criterion's own wording -
| #          a treatment that raises population has not destabilized anything -
| #          so the bound is one-sided by design.
| #   C9.5   duplication versus insertion. Descriptive, not pass/fail, as the
| #          criterion specifies. A10 against A10i and A1 against A1i, at
| #          matched duplication rates, reporting structural growth and the
| #          C9.2 outcome for each.
| #
| # TWO CONTROLS, because the criterion's phrase "a rate adjusted so total
| # expected value-change per genome per generation matches A" has two
| # defensible readings and picking one silently would hide a real choice.
| #   B      point mutation at the same 6554 the A conditions use: identical
| #          value-mutation, structural mutation absent. This is C9.1's
| #          instrument control and C9.2's stability baseline.
| #   Bload  point mutation at 13763 = A10's total operator rate
| #          (6554 point + 6554 duplication + 655 deletion): identical total
| #          mutational input, structural mutation absent. This separates
| #          "structure" from "mutation load".
| # If the two controls disagree, that difference is itself the finding.
