| # Phase 10 confirmatory campaign: C10.3 and C10.6.
| #
| # ============================================================
| # PRE-REGISTRATION. Committed before the campaign was run.
| # ============================================================
| #
| # ECOLOGY inherited from Phase 9's ecology sweep, which selected `k240` by a
| # rule fixed before it ran: highest median generation count subject to no
| # extinctions and population >= 200. Reused rather than re-swept because the
| # quantity it calibrates - generational turnover - is a property of the
| # ecology and not of the genome schema, and Phase 9 measured it on disjoint
| # seeds at this exact configuration. Morphology does change the phenotype,
| # so the regime is verified rather than assumed: `preflight` refuses any
| # world that cannot be built, and C10.6's own extinction count is reported.
| #
| # SEEDS: the first 30 in ascending order from 4001 that world generation
| # accepts, which is 4001..4012 and 4014..4031. Seed 4013 is refused - its
| # land fraction falls outside the configured bounds - and the selection rule
| # is **outcome-blind**: it depends only on terrain, before any condition is
| # applied, exactly as Phase 8's did. Preflight proves the declared design is
| # the one that runs, and it is what caught 4013 rather than the campaign
| # quietly executing as 29 seeds.
| #
| # Disjoint from every seed used anywhere in Phase 9 - the ecology sweep's
| # 1..8, the climate probe's 2002..2011, and the C9.1 confirmatory's
| # 3001..3030.
| #
| # THE MUTATION REGIME is Phase 9's A10 - duplication at ten times its shipped
| # default with deletion at default - because D-079 established that the
| # shipped rates produce no population-level structural change in 60,000
| # ticks, and morphology is expressed from the same loci. Running Phase 10 at
| # the shipped rates would reproduce that null and learn nothing new about
| # morphology. Transposition is 0: single-chromosome founders make it
| # provably inert (D-074).
| #
| # **THE RATE PROBLEM C10.3 HAS TO DESIGN AROUND, STATED FIRST.** D-080
| # measured that **89 percent of growth-rule mutations change nothing** - they
| # perturb a threshold a rule never reaches, or re-point a direction already
| # blocked. Morphological evolution under this encoding is therefore slow, and
| # a campaign that did not account for it would measure the effective
| # mutation rate rather than morphology, which is exactly what D-079 caught
| # C9.1 doing one phase earlier. Two things follow, both fixed here:
| #   - the run is 60,000 ticks, ~60 generations, the same budget C9.1 needed
| #     before its median moved at all;
| #   - the campaign reports its **realized** count of applied structural
| #     mutations and its morphology series, so a null can be attributed to
| #     "morphology did not matter" rather than to "morphology did not vary".
| #     A world whose morphology never diverged cannot speak to consequence
| #     and is reported as such rather than counted as a failure of C10.3.
| #
| # CONDITIONS
| #   M   evolvable morphology.
| #   F   fixed morphology: the growth program is present and inherited but
| #       **inert**, because `genome2.mutation.regulatory_enabled` is false.
| #       Every organism therefore develops the founder body - one digestive
| #       module - for the whole run. Matched to M on total mutational input:
| #       identical point, duplication and deletion rates, so the two differ in
| #       whether morphology *can* change and in nothing else.
| #   B   morphology disabled entirely: the Phase 9 world, phenotype from trait
| #       genes. Not a control for C10.3 - it is a different phenotype model -
| #       but it is the reference that says whether the morphology machinery
| #       costs anything ecologically at all.
| #
| # The F/M contrast is C10.3's and C10.6's control. The B arm is reported and
| # is not a criterion.
| #
| # DECISION RULES, fixed here before the run. C10.3 is **conjunctive over
| # three clauses** because ADR-0022 A13 says novelty is not progress: a
| # diverging module count on its own is variance, not consequence.
| #
| #   C10.3a  divergence. Under M, more than one distinct body among the
| #           living AND a median module count above the founder's 1, at the
| #           final tick.
| #   C10.3b  consequence. Under M, among organisms past maturity, the
| #           Spearman correlation between module count and cumulative
| #           offspring must exceed what the same world produces when the
| #           morphologies are **permuted** against the reproductive records.
| #           The permutation is the null: it destroys the morphology-outcome
| #           link while preserving both marginals and every age effect, which
| #           an unpaired comparison would not. Bar: the observed correlation
| #           must exceed the 95th percentile of 199 permutations.
| #   C10.3c  persistence. Under M, divergence present at the halfway sample
| #           (tick 30,000) is still present at the final sample, read from the
| #           `.almo` series. "Persists beyond the stated window" is a claim
| #           about a series, and a final tick alone cannot distinguish a
| #           durable shift from a transient one.
| #   C10.3   met when a, b, and c all hold in at least 20 of 30 worlds.
| #
| #   C10.6   stability. M against F, seed-paired: median population and
| #           median completed lifespan within 25 percent **or better**, each
| #           in at least 20 of 30 seeds. Same bound and same one-sided
| #           wording as C9.2, so the two phases' stability claims are
| #           directly comparable.
| #
| # CAPS PINNED EXPLICITLY, so a later revision of a default cannot move this
| # campaign's effective config - the coupling D-078 removed for Phase 9.
