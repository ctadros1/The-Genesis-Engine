Phase 22 findings: lineages under the other order
=================================================
Recorded 2026-09-03. ADR-0037; pre-registration
experiments/phase22-lineages-preregistration.md LOCKED before any
confirmatory world ran. Reduction:
experiments/results/phase22-lineages-reduction.py (counts, decides

The question
------------
Phase 20 found multi-module lineages in the shipped physics once per
six worlds (D-134) and named the term that limits them: a second module
arises only in born organisms, and a born organism reproduces with
probability ~0.07 before it starves. Phase 21 found what that term is
made of - the youngest eats last - and that youngest-first lifts the
born cohort's life tenfold (D-135). Is the order what limits lineages?

The pilot (an observation, not a result)
----------------------------------------
Phase 20's lineage census over Phase 21's confirmatory, both arms, 30
matched seeds 21002..21031: shipped order 61 multi-module organisms in
25 worlds, 3 parents, 2 second-generation organisms in 1 world;
youngest-first 313 in 26 worlds, 214 parents in 23 worlds, 244 second-
generation in 16 worlds, largest per-world count 77. That campaign was
pre-registered for lifespan, not lineage, so this decided nothing; it
set the rules below.

The rules, fixed before the run
-------------------------------
Two, conjunctive, on 50 matched seeds disjoint from every world read
so far: (1) the seed-paired directed count of worlds where O2 has a
second-generation organism and O1 none, at least 15 of 50 (an
unconditional binomial tail; false-positive rate 0.002 at a shipped
baseline of 0.16 and restated at the upper bound of the observed O1
rate; power 0.90 at 0.45, 0.99 at the pilot's 0.53, ~0.5 at 0.35 - so a
null reads as "fewer than nine worlds in twenty", never as no effect);
(2) the exact conditional sign test on the discordant pairs, one-sided
p below 0.01, which assumes nothing about either rate. The reduction,
the phase's one new artifact, reproduces the pilot exactly (1 of 30
and 2 pooled; 16 of 30, 244 pooled, largest 77; 16 directed, 1 reverse,
p = 1.4e-4) and refuses a manifest with one world's census line
removed. Reviewed by a three-lens adversarial workflow (23 findings, 6
confirmed and applied) before the lock.

Confirmatory (C22.1, C22.2, C22.3)
----------------------------------
experiments/phase22-lineages-confirmatory.campaign: O1 (the shipped
intake order) against O2 (youngest-first, the only difference), 50
matched seeds 22001..22052 without 22010 and 22028, 100,000 ticks,
events on; 100 worlds, 0 failed, 861 s at 6 workers; no world at the

cohort.txt and reduction.txt beside the manifest.

C22.1 (primary) MET on both rules.
  Rule 1: 31 of 50 seed pairs where youngest-first holds a second-
    generation two-module organism and the shipped order none (3
    reverse pairs), against the bar of 15. Its false-positive rate,
    restated as pre-registered at the upper bound of the observed
    shipped rate (8 of 50 -> 0.291), is 0.076 - above 0.01, which is
    exactly why the record required the second rule.
  Rule 2: the exact conditional sign test on the 34 discordant pairs,
    31 against 3, one-sided p = 3.8e-7 (bar 0.01). Assumption-free; it
    carries the decision.
  Beside them: worlds with any second-generation organism, shipped 8 of
  50 - Phase 20's 8 of 50 reproduced on fresh seeds - and youngest-first
  36 of 50; pooled 149 (largest per-world count 75) against 854
  (largest 153); the birth-normalized rate, median per world 0 against

C22.2 - the lineage's shape: multi-module organisms per world median 2

What the phase found, in one paragraph
--------------------------------------
On fifty matched seeds, reversing the order in which co-located
organisms eat - youngest first, a permutation of who takes first and
nothing else - produced a second-generation two-module organism in 31
worlds where the shipped order produced none, against 3 the other way;
lineages that the shipped physics founds once per six worlds it now
founds in seven of ten. The mechanism attributed is D-135's: the order
acts through the born cohort's lifespan, and this contrast does not
separate the order from any other route to a longer born life - the
order is what differs between the arms, and the born median beside the
count shows the term and its consequence together. Both orders are
equally unauthored and the shipped order stays shipped; what the
record now carries is narrow and true: in this ecology the shipped
intake order is a sufficient cause of the rarity of multi-module
lineages, and a single permutation of who eats first is a sufficient
cause of their forming.
