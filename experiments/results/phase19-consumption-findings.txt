Phase 19 findings: chemistry as food (coupling v2) - the unicell ecology
=======================================================================
Recorded 2026-09-03. ADR-0034; pre-registration
experiments/phase19-consumption-preregistration.md LOCKED before any
confirmatory or soak world ran (commit 4aa4a34). Reduction:
experiments/results/phase19-consumption-reduction.py (counts, decides
nothing). Runs archived under runs/phase19-consumption-pilot-
0x6201f977132973cc (8 worlds) and runs/phase19-consumption-

reduction.txt beside the manifest).

The question
------------
Phase 16 (D-131) ended with a materialized one-module unicell that
starves at ~200 ticks against a trait-derived maturity of 800, so its
C16.6 null was a birth-limit null. Phase 19 opens one mouth: after
biomass has had its turn, an organism may take substrate from its own
cell (S_MONOMER first, then S_PRIMORDIAL) into the digestive capability
it already has, at yield 0.6, with the loss returned to the cell as
waste and the gross taken counted in the field identity. The control is
coupling v1 - the Phase 16 world byte for byte - on the same seeds.

Campaign
--------
experiments/phase19-consumption-confirmatory.campaign: 64x64 scratch
worlds, 60,000 ticks, the Phase 16 confirmatory's base (transition at
defaults, floor 4,000, influx cap 64 per 100 ticks, pairing threshold
7,000, max_entities 4,000), two arms on 30 matched seeds
(19001..19008 19010..19022 19024..19032; 19009 and 19023 ungenerable
and replaced before any world ran): v1 (consumption fraction 0) and
v2 (Q16_ONE). 60 worlds, 0 failed, 302 s wall at 6 workers, the in-run
identity check every 5,000 ticks silent everywhere, and the reduction
re-deriving the field identity with the consumed term at all 120
samples of all 60 series with zero defect.

Pre-registered results
----------------------
C19.3 (primary) MET. Materialized median completed lifespan, v2 minus
  v1, seed-paired: 30 of 30 pairs above the SESOI of +200 ticks (bar 28
  of 30). Pair deltas: median +1,223.5, min +983, max +1,547.
    v1: median of the per-world medians 201 ticks (worlds 177-211);
        ~22,640 completed lifespans per world, ~150 censored.
    v2: 1,418.5 ticks (worlds 983+ above their pair); ~1,870 completed
        per world, ~720 censored (alive at the horizon, counted, never
        imputed - the completed median is if anything an underestimate).
  A fed unicell lives past its 800-tick maturity in every world. It
  still dies of starvation: the starvation share is 1,000 milli in all
  60 worlds. The mouth changes how long a life is, not what ends it.

C19.4 MET. Births per world, v2 minus v1: 30 of 30 pairs above the SESOI
  of +100 (bar 28 of 30). Deltas median +5,858.5, min +4,031, max
  +8,298. v1 median 5 births per world (D-131's rare births); v2 median
  5,866. The pre-registered arithmetic held: from 4,000 milli a fed gut
  reaches the 7,000 pairing threshold in ticks, so reproduction is
  ordinary under v2.

C19.5 (C16.6 re-asked) MET as pre-registered, and read carefully.
  Worlds with any body above one module (peak max_modules > 1 over the
  series): v2 17 of 30 (bar >= 15), v1 0 of 30 (bar <= 2). Beside it,
  as the pre-registration required: births v2 ~5,866 vs v1 ~5 - this is
  the null's release, not a null. What the peak hides: in every one of
  the 17 worlds the multi-module count never exceeded ONE organism at
  a time (peak multi_module 1), the first appearance is late (median
  sample 44,500 of 60,000; earliest 26,000), and at the final sample the
  multi-module count is 0 in most worlds (median 0, max 1). Peak
  max_modules is 2 everywhere it is above 1. So: the first bodies above
  one module appear once reproduction runs - and no multi-module lineage
  established itself inside the horizon. The mechanism reading (not a
  criterion): a materialized organism always starts with one module
  (the constant map), so every second module belongs to a born
  offspring, Phase 9's structural duplication (duplication_q16 6,554)
  acting on the first lineages that reproduce.

Free-lunch gate CLEAR. No world in either arm reached the entity cap
  (v2 final population median 911; v1 149.5). The field pays for the
  ecology visibly: final chem + microbial mass v2 median 86.4M milli
  against v1 407.2M (grazed to ~21%); consumed median 861.6M milli per
  v2 world, i.e. the organisms took ~1.75x the field's standing mass
  over the run. Materialization itself falls under v2 (median 2,587
  events vs 22,788): the field a fed population grazes condenses fewer
  new organisms, and the standing population is births, not influx.
  First materialization tick is identical between arms (median 24,500)
  - nothing eats before anyone exists.

Soak (C19.1)
------------
MET. experiments/phase19-c191-ledger-soak.campaign: one v2 scratch
world (seed 19201), 10^6 ticks, the full stack on, both identities
enforced by World::check_invariants every 10,000 ticks - 100 checks,
0 violations, 632 s wall - and exact at the horizon from the series:
  produced 8,192,000,000 + deposited 9,854,571,626
  - materialized 9,869,658 - consumed 17,952,388,401
  == chemistry 31,212,447 + microbial 53,101,120  (defect 0).
Over the run: 2,404 materializations, 149,352 births, 872 alive at the
end; the organisms took 2.2x the field's total production through the
mouth, and the loop closed to the milli. Archived at
runs/phase19-c191-soak-0x3d4af4da44ce3603.
The soak also extends C19.5's reading by a factor of seventeen in
horizon: over 10^6 ticks and 149,352 births, peak max_modules reached 2
exactly once (first at sample 940,000) and the final sample holds no
body above one module. Standing population ranged 159-1,120 across the
100 samples - the fed ecology oscillates rather than settles.

Cost (C19.8)
------------
RECORDED. scripts/run-phase19-benchmarks.sh (bench_phase19.rs, two
PHASE19-BENCH markers, medians over 200 ticks after 10 warm-up, each
arm timed after a throwaway world of the same shape so the first-world
cold-start cost does not masquerade as the flag's). At 64x64:
  consumption-tick: 16 organisms 139.0 us off -> 142.3 us on;
                    256 organisms 198.8 us off -> 204.6 us on;
                    diff-in-diff ~0.011 us (~11 ns) per organism-tick.
  field-cell (no organisms): 105.0 ns per cell off, 102.0 ns on - the
                    difference is inside run-to-run noise and its sign
                    flips between runs.
The mouth is priced per organism-tick at the tens-of-nanoseconds level
and the field's cost stays per-cell, as the plan required. The
biomass-off arm of the populated benchmark is fed only from the field,
so the loop it prices is the one that actually runs.
