1. C13.1 - THE PRIMARY CONTRASTS
----------------------------------------------------------------

Per world, F = mean naive arrival fraction over epochs 10, 15, 20, 25
(window [2000e, 2000e+2000), naive cut at 2000e, censoring counted never
imputed). Seed-paired between-arm differences, direction increase, SESOI
20 milli absolute, decided on reaching_absolute_directed (the relative
form is None at a zero control fraction - D-120):

  A-vs-C  pairs=30 unusable=0 reaching=0/30 p=1.000 positive=11
          mean=-4 milli  ci95=[-8,-1]  median=-3
  A-vs-D  pairs=30 unusable=0 reaching=0/30 p=1.000 positive=14
          mean=-1 milli  ci95=[-5,+1]  median=-2

Not one seed of thirty reached +20 milli in either contrast; the 95%
intervals localize any true effect below ~+1 milli, twenty times smaller
than the SESOI. The point estimates lean slightly negative (the A arm
arrives marginally *slower*); the A-vs-C interval excludes zero on the
negative side by 1 milli, which at this resolution is reported as
estimation, not evidence of harm.

Why this is a transmission null and not a reachability null (the
pre-registration's own reading note, applied): in the epoch-25 census,
every single A world has hearers (min 331), speakers (min 57), organisms
that both emit and receive (min 19), and a naive cohort in the hundreds
(min 634). Signals flow at scale - median 7.0M priced emissions per A
world - and are delivered on time under Rule 4's one-tick lag. The
channel is built, populated, paid for, and used; what it carries does
not move naive organisms toward the patch any faster than silence.

Zero worlds extinct, zero perception faults across all 240 worlds.

**Speaker depletion under billing.** The arms where emission is priced
(A, D, S: ~520-550 median speakers) carry 2.5x FEWER speakers than the
arms where the signal channel is disabled and the same genes cost
nothing (B: 1,388; C: 1,413). Emission cost is selecting against
speakers wherever it is actually billed. This is the social-mechanisms
review's "signals become noise" risk realized in its evolutionary form -
the channel does not fill with lies, it empties of speakers - and it is
the single strongest lever candidate the campaign surfaced: at this
pricing, selection removes the transmitter before transmission can pay.

**The S ablation verified at scale.** S is the only arm with a zero
median for expressed rule-5 organisms (every other arm 7-16); its
`rule5_updates_total == 0` gate held in all 30 worlds. The
observational-rule knob does what it says under campaign densities, not
just in the Gate E fixture.

never_observed medians run 28k-31k in all arms - dominated by
post-window births, bookkeeping not signal, per the locked reading note.

3. C13.2 - THE F-CURVE IS FLAT, AND THE NO-CHANNEL ARMS EXPLAIN IT
----------------------------------------------------------------

Median per-world fidelity delta (exposed-pair policy similarity minus
same-kinship-bin matched-control similarity, milli), ~20,600 exposed
pairs per world, zero worlds unusable:

  corruption      0      8192     16384     32768
  delta        +15.0    +13.0     +16.0     +14.0     [the curve]
  B (no signal)         +19.0
  C (no perception)     +17.0                         [no-channel context]
  D (scrambled)         +17.5
  S (no rule 5)         +14.5

The curve does not bend across a corruption sweep spanning half the Q16
range - and the arms with NO channel at all sit at the same +15-19
milli as the arm with the full channel. Whatever the exposure delta
measures, it is present in full when nothing can be transmitted;
subtracting the no-channel baseline, the transmission-attributable
fidelity is indistinguishable from zero at every corruption level. The
residual +15-19 milli common to all arms is spatial assortment finer
than the kinship bins - organisms co-located with a demonstrator
resemble it for reasons the four relatedness bins do not fully absorb.

The persistence>1 line, computed from the run's own delivery counts as
the locked rule requires: R = 5,307 median deliveries per hearer at
epoch 25 under A, putting the line at 1000/R = 0.188 milli. The raw
deltas sit far above that line, but the line is reported as context
only (the rule borrowed no threshold): the as-built F is a similarity
delta, not a per-event copy probability, and the no-channel arms show
the delta is not transmission at all. No accumulation claim is
licensed. Control power is visible in the per-bin tables in the
committed fidelity report (unmatched-exposed counts concentrate in the
closest-kin bin, where control candidates are scarcest).

4. C13.3 - THE TRADITION DETECTOR'S CONTROL ARM CONVICTS IT
----------------------------------------------------------------

A: 8 of 30 worlds with findings (bar 15) - unmet on its own. But the
sharper result is the control arm: C shows 4 of 12 worlds with findings
against a locked bar of ZERO. A world with perception and signalling
both disabled cannot host a socially transmitted variant, so every C
finding is a false positive by construction, and at a ~30% world rate
the A-arm positives cannot be read as traditions either. Findings per
arm across all eight (A 9, B 8, C 11, D 9, S 5, A8k 6, A16k 4, A32k 18
total findings) are consistent with one channel-independent generator.

What passes the detector: variants concentrated in a quadrat at both
endpoints of a >3-lifespan gap with zero shared members, exceeding
their frequency in a genotype-matched cohort elsewhere. What the
uniform arm profile says is that this world's physics produces such
configurations from inheritance plus spatial structure alone - the
genotype-matched cohort control, as specified, does not fully absorb
local lineage clustering. Of A's 56,929 candidates, 44,320 died at the
endpoint-persistence requirement and 12,595 at member turnover; zero
died for want of a cohort and zero at the control - the control is not
starved, it is insufficiently sharp. C13.3 is UNMET, and the era-and-
tradition spec needs a stronger control (a within-lineage placebo or a
permutation over quadrat assignments) before its verdicts mean
anything. Recorded as a methods finding, not tuned post hoc.

8. C13.8 / C13.9 / C13.10 - THE CONFLICT ADDENDUM'S READINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------

**C13.8 (persistent communities):** A 10/30 worlds with persistent
chains above their proximity-matched null p95 (bar 20); C 10/30 -
exactly arm-symmetric, chains median 0 and max 2 in both arms. The
pilot's marginal-to-null expectation held. Community persistence in
this physics is scarce and cue-independent; what "community" the
detector finds is home-range structure, and the null comparison - the
criterion's own "not spatial proximity alone" clause - keeps it from
being reported as more.

**C13.9 (directed aggression, the addendum's primary):** seed-paired
A-vs-C contrast of the co-present between/within factor, SESOI 500
milli absolute increase: 6/30 reaching (bar 20), exact binomial
p = 1.000, mean difference -234 milli, median -138, positive 13/30.
The expected null, exactly as the pilot's cross-arm symmetry
predicted. Descriptively both arms show elevated between-community
attack rates on co-present opportunities (A median factor 1,572 milli,
16/30 >= 1,500; C median 1,816, 21/30) - boundary structure present
without perception, deciding nothing, per the addendum's own clause.

**C13.10 (coalition and asymmetry):** the pre-stated expected null,
and the arms agree to within rounding. Between-community attacks with
local numerical advantage vs disadvantage: A 5,226 vs 2,819 (1.85:1),
C 6,059 vs 3,348 (1.81:1) - attackers are where their community is,
in both arms equally, so the asymmetry is spatial, not social.
Coalition targets (>= 2 attackers in-window): A 48 milli of attacked
targets, C 47 milli. Nothing here depends on the channel; any stronger
claim needs its own pre-registration.
