READING, AGAINST THE LOCKED EXPECTATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE PERSISTENCE CEILING, AS PRE-REGISTERED. Every condition,
   including neutral N, carries a persistent self-sustaining microbial
   population in 30/30 worlds: formation fires everywhere (median ~130
   firings per 10^6 cell-ticks), the standing density is ~53x
   everything abiogenesis ever seeded (growth-dominated, not
   re-seeding-dominated), and every cell of every world is occupied at
   the horizon. The persistence curve against scaffold intensity is
   FLAT AT THE CEILING, and per the pre-registration the flat curve is
   the reported result. The phase plan's a-priori expectation - that
   under N abiogenesis never fires usefully or does not persist - DOES
   NOT HOLD at the shipped rate constants; the pre-registration
   recorded this from the fixture before the campaign, and the campaign
   confirms it at scale. It is a fact about the chosen rate regime
   (growth 2-6% per step against 1% death over an accumulating
   substrate), not a tuned outcome: nothing was adjusted after seeing
   the data.

2. THE CONTRAST DIRECTION IS MEASURABLE AND SMALL. Concentrating the
   same production into patches LOWERS total formation slightly (about
   -1.86 per 10^6 cell-ticks, -1.4% versus N) and RAISES sustainment
   slightly (+0.65..+0.69 on 52.7, about +1.3%). At equal production
   totals, patchiness trades a little formation for a little
   per-seeded-milli sustainment; both differences are far from the
   persistence margin at these rates. A plausible mechanism - patch
   cells sitting at the abiogenesis rate cap while off-patch substrate
   thins - is consistent with the numbers but was not separately
   instrumented; it is a hypothesis, not a finding.

3. INSTRUMENT FINDING: THE CONTRAST LEVER SATURATES BY 4x. On all 30
   seeds, the S2 (4x), S3 (8x) and S4 (16x) worlds are BYTE-IDENTICAL
   trajectories - every field series sample equal, every recorded total
   equal; their state checksums differ only because state_checksum
   folds in config_hash and the stored contrast word differs. The
   integer production-weight redistribution evidently floors the
   background share to the same value at contrast >= 4x with radius 3
   on a 64x64 map, so "4x", "8x" and "16x" are all the same
   fully-concentrated arm. The intended five-point curve is effectively
   three points: uniform, 2x, fully-concentrated. ANY FUTURE SCAFFOLD
   SWEEP at this radius/map must sweep below 4x (or vary radius) to
   place more than one interior point on the curve. Recorded as a
   methods finding; the three distinct points still bracket the lever's
   whole range, so C15.3's curve-with-controls requirement is met.

4. C15.4 NAMING TEST. The scaffold description - "the same substrate
   input concentrated into patches of radius 3 at contrast c" - names
   an environmental structure and no outcome. The campaign's conditions
   differ from N only in the two scaffold fields (the vary declaration
   and per-world config hashes agree).
