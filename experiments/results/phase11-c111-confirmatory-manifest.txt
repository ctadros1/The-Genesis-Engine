| # Phase 11 confirmatory campaign: C11.1 and C11.2.
| #
| # ============================================================
| # PRE-REGISTRATION. Committed before the campaign was run.
| # ============================================================
| #
| # WHAT THIS CAMPAIGN IS FOR. C11.1 and C11.2 have stood at NOT MEASURED
| # since the phase opened, and `planning/phase-11-lifetime-learning.md` is
| # explicit that this is different from unmet: nothing was observed and no
| # threshold was tested. Four things had to exist first. Three now do - the
| # relocating resource patch (Phase 12's mutable-world half), per-organism
| # action counting, and a neutral marker locus - and the fourth, the analysis
| # that reduces a per-individual shift to a world-level rate, is
| # `sim-analysis::plasticity`, whose bars are restated below and echoed into
| # every report it prints.
| #
| # THE PHASE'S OWN PRIOR IS UNFAVOURABLE AND IS RECORDED HERE RATHER THAN
| # DISCOVERED LATER. The Risks table names "plasticity is selected to zero
| # and the phase returns a null result" as the single most likely failure of
| # this phase, and two measurements already point that way: 1 plastic edge
| # across 500 organisms after 30,000 ticks, and the founders' 400 plastic
| # edges gone by 10^6. This campaign exists to give those numbers a control
| # and a threshold, not to overturn them. A null here is a result and will be
| # reported as one.
| #
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| # CONDITIONS. Two factors crossed, four arms, 30 seeds each.
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| #
| #   Avar  plasticity on,  E-variable  (patch relocates and its magnitude bites)
| #   Astat plasticity on,  E-stationary (patch relocates at zero magnitude)
| #   Bvar  plasticity off, E-variable
| #   Bstat plasticity off, E-stationary
| #
| # THE PLASTICITY FACTOR is two fields and both are set together:
| # `plasticity.enabled` (the learn phase, the learned-state section and the
| # per-plastic-edge energy cost) and `genome2.mutation.plasticity_enabled`
| # (whether point mutation can reach `eta` and `EDGE_FLAG_PLASTIC` at all).
| # Under B every founder's `eta` is 0.0 and no mutation can move it, so
| # behaviour can change only across generations - the plan's own wording. The
| # mutation gate **consumes its draws either way** (D-086), so the two arms
| # are matched on total mutational input and differ only in what the draws are
| # allowed to write.
| #
| # THE ENVIRONMENT FACTOR IS A ZERO-MAGNITUDE SCHEDULE, NOT A SCHEDULE-FREE
| # WORLD. This is the single most important control in the design and it is
| # not the obvious one. Relocating a patch trims biomass into a ledgered loss
| # sink every time capacity leaves a cell, so an arm with no schedule would
| # carry a different standing biomass for a reason that has nothing to do with
| # learning. At `patch_capacity_scale_q16 = 65536` the schedule runs
| # identically - same draws, same override set, same entry count, same code
| # path - and composes to exactly the baseline capacity, so E-stationary and
| # E-variable are matched on everything except the magnitude of the move.
| # `the_zero_magnitude_control_runs_the_identical_schedule_and_loses_no_biomass`
| # asserts the control's loss sink stays at zero.
| #
| # WHY THE B ARM IS INDISPENSABLE, STATED BEFORE THE RESULT. The founder
| # genome binds input channel 1 (`energy_fraction`) through one hidden node to
| # output channel 101 (`turn`). An organism's turn intent therefore moves
| # whenever its energy moves - with or without plasticity, and a relocating
| # patch moves energy. So **an action-distribution change across a relocation
| # is expected under B as well**, and it is not evidence of lifetime learning:
| # it is the same fixed policy emitting different actions because the input
| # changed. C11.1's claim is that plasticity produced the change, so the
| # criterion is conjunctive: the treatment must clear the bar **and the
| # control must not**. If both clear it, C11.1 is unmet, and the honest
| # reading is that the world showed stimulus-driven behavioural change and
| # nothing this campaign can attribute to learning.
| #
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| # SEEDS
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| #
| # The first 30 in ascending order from 6001 that world generation accepts,
| # which at this map size is 6001..6030 with **no exclusions**: preflight over
| # 6001..6060 at this exact config refused none of them. The rule is stated in
| # the same outcome-blind form Phase 8 and Phase 10 used - it depends only on
| # terrain, before any condition is applied - because a rule that is only
| # written down when it has to exclude something is not a rule. Preflight is
| # still run and still fails the campaign closed if it refuses anything, which
| # is what stops a declared 30-seed design quietly executing as a 25-seed one.
| #
| # Disjoint from every seed used anywhere else: Phase 9's 1..8, 2002..2011 and
| # 3001..3030; Phase 10's 4001..4031; this phase's own pilot at 9001..9004 and
| # the CLI test's 5101..5103.
| #
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| # TWO CALIBRATIONS TAKEN FROM A PILOT, AND THE RULE EACH USED
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| #
| # Both were fixed on pilot seeds 9001..9004, which are disjoint from the set
| # above, and both rules are stated in terms of a **control** rather than of
| # either criterion's outcome.
| #
| # 1. THE POINT-MUTATION RATE IS 65535, NOT THE SHIPPED 6554. Rule: the
| #    neutral marker's alleles must actually move, because a drift control
| #    that never drifts cannot bound drift and C11.2 would be undefined in
| #    every world for a reason that is about the mutation rate rather than
| #    about plasticity. Measured on the pilot at 20,000 ticks: at 6554, **0**
| #    marker alleles had left the founder value across the four A-worlds; at
| #    65535, **126**. This is D-079 and D-080's lesson applied before the fact
| #    - at shipped rates the measured outcome is the effective mutation rate,
| #    not the mechanism - and it is the same reason Phase 10 ran duplication
| #    at ten times its default. `point_delta_q16` is pinned at its documented
| #    3,277 because C11.2's threshold is computed from it.
| #
| # 2. THE PATCH IS RADIUS 32 AND SCALE 4.0, NOT THE DEFAULT 15 AND 2.0. Rule:
| #    the schedule must measurably change the world, or E-variable is an
| #    inert treatment and the whole environmental factor is decoration. At
| #    radius 32 the footprint is 4,225 cells of a 16,384-cell map before the
| #    habitable filter, and on all four pilot seeds the E-variable arm ends
| #    with a higher population than its seed-matched E-stationary arm (5,363
| #    against 2,993; 1,353 against 1,156; 2,575 against 1,000; 1,621 against
| #    1,155) and a different standing biomass (higher in three, lower in one).
| #    This is a property of the environment, measured in the A and B arms
| #    alike, and it does not depend on either criterion's statistic.
| #    `worldmod.max_capacity_overrides` is raised to 8,192 so the larger
| #    footprint fits inside the per-layer cap rather than being silently
| #    clipped.
| #
| # WHAT THE PILOT ALSO SHOWED, DISCLOSED HERE RATHER THAN AFTER THE FACT: the
| # association between a relocation and an individual's behavioural change ran
| # **negative** in both arms - behaviour was slightly *more* stable across a
| # relocation than across a matched boundary inside one epoch. The directed
| # decision rule below is retained unchanged. C11.1's claim is that plasticity
| # produces behavioural change in response to the event; an event that makes
| # behaviour more stable is an association but it is not that claim. The
| # two-sided count is reported for every condition so a sign-reversed
| # association is visible rather than hidden, and it decides nothing.
| #
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| # THE MEASUREMENT
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| #
| # A relocation happens on every tick that is a multiple of
| # `relocate_interval_ticks` (2,000). Action samples are written every 500
| # ticks, so 2,000 / 2 = 1,000 is a whole number of sample windows and the
| # matched control boundary lands exactly on a sample.
| #
| # For each relocation tick T and each organism present at all six sample
| # ticks it needs, with the ages to prove it lived every tick between them:
| #
| #   event pair    windows [T-500, T] and [T, T+500]        - straddles the move
| #   control pair  windows [T+500, T+1000] and [T+1000, T+1500] - inside one epoch
| #
| # Each window's seven counts are normalized by that window's own locomotion
| # total, in milli, and the distance is the L1 over the normalized vector.
| # The locomotion columns partition the tick so half their L1 is a genuine
| # total variation between two behaviour policies; the three indicators are
| # independent and are reported separately. Everything is integer.
| #
| # The world statistic is the rank correlation between "was this boundary a
| # relocation" and the distance, over every (individual, boundary) observation
| # after burn-in. The null shuffles the labels 199 times and takes the 95th
| # percentile of the absolute correlation. That destroys the event-distance
| # link while preserving both marginals - Phase 10's permutation argument and
| # Phase 10's code.
| #
| # A world whose distances never vary is reported as `no_variance` and counted
| # separately, never as a failure. This is not a formality: Phase 11's own
| # substrate measurement found `Eat` and `Mate` saturated and `Rest` and
| # `TurnLeft` empty in a founder-dominated world, so a degenerate controller
| # could otherwise stand in for a refutation of lifetime learning. Every
| # report line carries the per-column occupancy so a null can be attributed to
| # the right cause.
| #
| # C11.2 reads the final genomes. `eta` and `EDGE_FLAG_PLASTIC` both start at
| # the founder value - 0.0 and clear - and the neutral marker locus starts at
| # exactly the same two values, mutates on exactly the same draws with exactly
| # the same delta and clamp, sits between the two founder edges so it is as
| # tightly linked to each as they are to each other, and is never expressed.
| # Its mutation is deliberately **not** gated on
| # `genome2.mutation.plasticity_enabled`, so the control drifts in the B arm
| # too and the B arm measures drift with plasticity provably frozen. Both
| # quantities are averaged per organism first and then across organisms, so a
| # genome that duplicated its way to forty edges does not outvote thirty
| # founders (ADR-0022 A5, one level down).
| #
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| # DECISION RULES AND EVERY THRESHOLD, FIXED HERE BEFORE THE RUN
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| #
| #   burn_in_ticks         6000   three whole relocation epochs
| #   sample interval       500    ticks between action samples
| #   relocation interval   2000   ticks between patch moves
| #   min_individuals       8      distinct organisms before a world's
| #                                correlation is read at all
| #   permutations          199    so the 95th percentile is the exact 190th
| #                                order statistic, with no interpolation
| #   drift_margin_milli    25     one expected mutational step: a point
| #                                mutation's delta is uniform on +/- 0.05 of
| #                                the value's range at the pinned
| #                                point_delta_q16 = 3277, so E|delta| = 25
| #                                milli. An excess smaller than one typical
| #                                step is not an effect worth naming.
| #   analysis_seed         0x9e3779b97f4a7c15
| #
| #   C11.1  Under Avar, in at least **20 of 30** worlds, the signed rank
| #          correlation between the relocation label and the per-individual
| #          window distance must exceed the 95th percentile of its own
| #          permutation null. Under Bvar the same count must be **below 20**.
| #          Met only if both hold.
| #
| #   C11.2  Under Avar, in at least **20 of 30** worlds, either the mean `eta`
| #          or the plastic-allele fraction must exceed the neutral marker's
| #          matched quantity by at least 25 milli. Under Bvar the same count
| #          must be **below 20**. Either quantity, not both: `eta` and
| #          `EDGE_FLAG_PLASTIC` are two independently reachable fields of one
| #          mechanism, and requiring both would make the criterion a test of
| #          which mutation target happened to be hit - C9.1's "nodes or
| #          edges" reasoning.
| #
| # The Astat and Bstat arms are reported in full and decide nothing. They are
| # the environmental control: if Avar clears the bar, Astat is what says
| # whether the relocation schedule's magnitude was necessary.
| #
| # NO THRESHOLD ABOVE MAY BE CHANGED AFTER THE DATA IS SEEN. A null is a
| # result.
