PHASE 11 REACHABILITY 2x2: FINDINGS
===================================

Campaign phase11-reachability, hash 0x8efbcf9694206dc6, 48 worlds, 0 failed,
60,000 ticks each, 4,651.7 wall seconds on genesis-engine (x86_64 Linux, 8
workers). Built at commit 974cb7d. Pre-registration:
experiments/phase11-reachability-preregistration.md, committed before the run.

Arms: N (neither), C (chain only), M (moat only), B (both). 12 seeds each,
matched across arms.


1. THE PRE-REGISTERED DECISION, APPLIED
---------------------------------------

Bar, fixed in advance: the arm's `full_conjunction` count exceeds N's in at
least 10 of 12 seed-matched pairs. Ties count as failures.

On EXPRESSED plastic edges - the measure section 6's power calculation was
built on:

    C vs N   10 of 12 wins, 1 loss,  1 tie    MEETS the bar
    B vs N   11 of 12 wins, 1 loss,  0 ties   MEETS the bar
    M vs N    4 of 12 wins, 5 losses, 3 ties  FAILS

On EDGE ALLELES:

    C vs N    3 of 12    FAILS
    M vs N    1 of 12    FAILS
    B vs N    1 of 12    FAILS

**These do not conflict, and section 2 explains why the allele measure cannot
answer this question.** No threshold was changed. C meets the bar at exactly
10 of 12, with no margin, and that is stated rather than rounded up.


2. THE ALLELE MEASURE IS BLIND TO THE CHAIN, BY CONSTRUCTION
------------------------------------------------------------

The pre-registration's section 3 named "edge alleles, and separately expressed
plastic edges" as the primary outcome. **That was a defect in the
pre-registration and it is mine.** The two measures cannot both answer this,
and which one could was knowable before the run from ADR-0027.

ADR-0027 applies the chain's remap in `compile_with_budget`, and deliberately
leaves `PlasticityGenes::normalized` reducing mod 5. The allele census reads
the normalized genotype; the expressed census reads the compiled plan. So the
chain is invisible at the allele level by design.

Measured, and this is the discriminating check rather than an argument:

    allele-level non_static     N 6.42%   C 6.62%   M 6.22%   B 5.90%
    expressed non_static        N ~7%     C 100%    M ~7%     B 100%

The chain does not move the allele-level marginal at all, in either arm that
has it on. An instrument that cannot see the factor cannot test it.

Section 6 - the power calculation that justifies the 10-of-12 bar - is
explicitly derived "working from the expressed histogram". The bar was
therefore powered for the expressed measure and never for the allele one.
Reporting the expressed result as the answer is faithful to the document; the
allele result is reported beside it and is uninformative for C and B.

**What should have happened:** section 3 should have named one measure, and
noticing which required only reading ADR-0027 next to it. Recorded so the next
pre-registration checks that its outcome measure can observe its factor.


3. THE EFFECT SIZE MATCHES THE PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION
---------------------------------------------------------

    predicted   10.7x   (0.0888 x 0.1476 x 48,119 = 631 against 59)
    observed    13.0x   (468 against 36)

The prediction was made before the run from the published census's marginals,
and its reasoning was that `rule != 0` is the binding condition at 6.54
percent rather than a 4-in-5 lottery. That reasoning holds.

Per world, count and max beside the mean (D-098):

    N   mean  3.0   max  21   (one world carries 21 of 36)
    C   mean 39.0   max 266   (one world carries 266 of 468)

**Both arms are concentrated and neither total should be read alone.** C wins
10 of 12 pairs, so its result is not carried by its largest world - but its
total is.


4. THE MOAT FAILS ITS BAR, AND DOES SOMETHING ELSE ENTIRELY
------------------------------------------------------------

M reaches 4 of 12 and its total is *lower* than N's (19 against 36). Pricing
only moved edges does not improve reachability and may slightly reduce it.

What it does do is exactly what it was built to do:

    plasticity cost, milli-EU
      N  100,568,219        M      40,895      2,459x cheaper
      C   98,997,904        B     377,449        262x cheaper

The moat drains the moat. It does not shorten the chain, and the 2x2 is what
separates those two claims - which is why D-107 insisted on it.


5. MECHANISM CONFIRMATIONS
---------------------------

`static` steps, which are the rule-0 early return:

    N  479,266,538      C  0      M  468,980,336      B  0

Zero in both chain arms. The chain removes rule 0 completely, as designed.

Learned state actually moved:

    nonzero_learned    N   8    C 448    M  15    B 138
    max_abs_q16        N 15,054  C 441,275  M 20,773  B 131,000

Learning is 56x more common under the chain than without it.


6. THE HEBBIAN CONCENTRATION, PREDICTED AND MEASURED
-----------------------------------------------------

ADR-0027 predicted that under the chain the founder's untouched allele maps to
LIVE_RULE_BASE, so the standing population would be about 92 percent plain
Hebbian, and made reporting this an obligation. Measured:

    compiled rule histogram
      N   rule0 92.4%  rule1  1.7%  rule2 1.6%  rule3 2.2%  rule4 2.1%
      M   rule0 94.9%  rule1  1.6%  rule2 1.4%  rule3 0.8%  rule4 1.3%
      C   rule0  0.0%  rule1 92.5%  rule2 1.3%  rule3 3.0%  rule4 3.2%
      B   rule0  0.0%  rule1 94.7%  rule2 2.2%  rule3 1.8%  rule4 1.2%

92.5 and 94.7 percent against a predicted "about 92". The prediction was made
before the campaign and is confirmed.

**And the obligation earned itself.** Clamp saturations:

    N 0      C 733      M 0      B 0

C is the only arm with saturations, and it is the arm with the chain and
without the moat: every edge pays, nearly every edge is Hebbian, and the
bounded Hebbian rule reaches its clamp 733 times. Without this number on the
record, C's result would read as "learning works" rather than as "learning
works, and the arm is 92 percent plain Hebbian and has started hitting the
bound". `LEARN_LIMIT_Q16` is doing work here, and a future campaign that
removed the clamp would be measuring something different.


7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SUPPORT
---------------------------------------

Supported, on the pre-registered bar and measure:

  - The chain shortens the conjunction and makes the phenotype reachable:
    C 10 of 12, B 11 of 12, with a 13.0x effect close to the 10.7x predicted
    in advance.
  - D-107's reading of Phase 11's null as *reachability* rather than
    selection is strengthened. The conjunction was not being selected away;
    it was not being assembled, and removing one condition assembles it.

NOT supported:

  - Any claim that plasticity is now *useful*. This campaign measures whether
    the genotype and the compiled phenotype exist, not whether carrying them
    pays. Payoff was deliberately not the endpoint (D-100 and C11.1's second
    confound make the behavioural statistic unsafe), and nothing here speaks
    to it.
  - Any claim about the moat improving reachability. It fails its bar.
  - Any interaction claim. None was tested; at 12 seeds on this count it
    would have no power, and B is reported against N on the same bar as the
    others. That B (11 of 12) and C (10 of 12) are close, while B's learned
    state is lower than C's (138 against 448), is descriptive only.

Limitations carried from the pre-registration: no multiplicity correction
across the three bars; no power claim for the moat arms; E-variable held on
rather than crossed; 12 seeds.

One limitation not in the pre-registration, found here: the campaign was built
at 974cb7d, which carries a `stable_hash` regression fixed in 2e1d298. It
cannot affect these results - `stable_hash` is provenance and is never read by
the tick, and every arm sets `plasticity.enabled true`, where both versions of
the block are identical - but it is recorded rather than omitted.
