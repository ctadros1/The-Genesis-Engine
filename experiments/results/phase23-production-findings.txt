The question
------------
The shipped intake order - the youngest eats last - is a sufficient
cause of the born cohort's short life and of the rarity of multi-
module lineages, and a permutation of who eats first removes both
(D-135, D-136). The record kept the shipped order: both orders are
equally unauthored. What physics can answer is why the order matters:
the field produces 2 milli per cell per step at one step per tick and
a fed unicell takes up to 200 per tick, so a cell an elder has emptied
refills at one percent of a single take per tick and a newborn beside
it finds nothing for a hundred ticks. On the field's own terms: at
what production does the order stop mattering - and does production
alone, under the shipped order, carry the born cohort to where the
other order carried it?

The arithmetic, stated before the run
-------------------------------------
Doses P1 2 (shipped, the control), P4 8, P16 32, P64 128, P128 256,
P256 512 milli per cell per step: refill against a single take of 1,
4, 16, 64, 128 and 256 percent per tick. P128 is the first rung at
which a cell refills within a tick for one eater; a newborn's cell
refills for it only after every elder sharing the cell has taken.
The other order runs beside the shipped one at the shipped dose (P1D,
Phase 21's O2 on these seeds - the reference measured rather than
quoted) and at the top dose (PTD - the order contrast at the dose
where the arithmetic says it should stop mattering). The reduction,
the phase's one new analysis artifact, reproduces the Phase 21
archive exactly (born medians 222 against 2,191; 30 of 30 pairs, delta
median 1,969, interval [1,944, 2,007]; field mass, materialized counts
and occupants as pinned in ADR-0038) and refuses a removed line, an
archive read without its pin flag, a broken field identity or a
materialized count that differs between census, manifest and series.
Production was swept once before, in Phase 15 (D-128), where the
field's persistence saturated by fourfold.

The harness change
------------------
The campaign manifest's run line now carries the transition's three
counters (transition_materialized, transition_deferred_cap,
transition_deferred_capacity). Before this, the entity-cap gate could
see only births refused at the cap; a dose whose cap bound through
materialization alone was invisible to every campaign artifact.
Additive, absent-tolerant, every archived manifest still parses;
wiring test against a solo run's metrics under two caps.
