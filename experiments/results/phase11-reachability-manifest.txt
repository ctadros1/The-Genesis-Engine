| # Phase 11 reachability 2x2: chain, moat, both, neither.
| #
| # ============================================================
| # PRE-REGISTRATION. Committed before the campaign is run.
| # The design, the power calculation, the decision rule and every
| # reporting obligation are in
| # experiments/phase11-reachability-preregistration.md, committed first
| # and separately. This file is that document's executable form and
| # adds nothing to it.
| # ============================================================
| #
| # WHAT THIS CAMPAIGN IS FOR. D-107 established that Phase 11's null is about
| # reachability rather than selection: the phase's risk table predicted
| # "plasticity is selected to zero", and what actually happened is that
| # plasticity was never assembled. Two changes were proposed, and D-107 was
| # explicit that they are different claims that must not be conflated. "X but
| # not Y" requires building the X-alone variant and measuring it, so this is a
| # 2x2 and not a single combined change.
| #
| #   N  chain off, moat off   today's engine; the arm the census describes
| #   C  chain on,  moat off   ADR-0027: every rule_id names a live rule
| #   M  chain off, moat on    D-111: only edges whose learned state moved pay
| #   B  chain on,  moat on
| #
| # THE PRIMARY OUTCOME IS REACHABILITY, NOT PAYOFF. `lifesim conjunction`'s
| # `full_conjunction` count. It counts genotypes, so it is sound regardless of
| # D-100 and of C11.1's second confound - both are about a *behavioural*
| # statistic and neither touches a census of which alleles exist. No claim
| # about within-lifetime behavioural shift is made here, and C11.1's statistic
| # is not used.
| #
| # THE BAR, FIXED BEFORE THE RUN AND NOT WEAKENABLE AFTER IT. Arm C's
| # `full_conjunction` count exceeds arm N's in at least 10 of 12 seed-matched
| # pairs; the same bar for M against N and for B against N. Ties count as
| # failures, which is the conservative direction and the one that matters when
| # the base rate is near zero. Under a null, 10-or-more of 12 is 79/4096 =
| # 0.0193, a one-sided sign test at about alpha = 0.02.
| #
| # WHY 12 SEEDS IS ENOUGH, AND WHY THE OBVIOUS ESTIMATE SAYS IT IS NOT. A
| # uniform-draw model says `rule_id != 0` has probability 4/5, so the chain
| # multiplies completions by 1.25 - undetectable here, and this campaign would
| # be a waste. That model does not describe this population. The census
| # measures 93.46 percent of compiled plastic edges carrying rule 0, because
| # the founder carries it and the mutation rate is low. `rule != 0` is the
| # BINDING condition at 6.54 percent, and the chain sets it to 1. The chain's
| # completion set becomes exactly {eta > 0} and {drive != 0}: under
| # independence 0.0888 x 0.1476 x 48,119 = 631 against an observed 59, a
| # predicted ~10.7x increase. The independence assumption is checkable on the
| # same table and roughly holds (predicts depth 3 = 1,316 against 1,477).
| #
| # NO POWER CLAIM IS MADE FOR THE MOAT. Its effect is on selection, not on the
| # mutational path, and there is no prior measurement to size it from. The M
| # and B arms are exploratory on this endpoint and are reported as such.
| #
| # NO INTERACTION IS TESTED. At 12 seeds on a count this sparse it would have
| # no useful power, and pretending otherwise would be a weakened threshold in
| # another form. B is reported against N on the same bar as the other two, and
| # any statement about whether the chain and the moat compose is descriptive.
| #
| # REPORTING OBLIGATIONS, and each exists because a specific reading went wrong
| # before:
| #
| #   - The per-world distribution in every arm, never only the arm total. In
| #     the census 6 of 9 allele-level completions came from one world.
| #   - The depth histogram at both the allele and expressed level. The chain
| #     moves alleles *between* depths, so a total that moved without the
| #     histogram moving as the mechanism predicts needs explaining.
| #   - The plastic flag's excess over MARKER_FLAG_NEUTRAL in every arm. Under
| #     the moat the flag is free to drift - that is the intended change and
| #     also exactly how the flag stops being informative - and the marker is
| #     the matched drift control because it toggles on the same draw.
| #   - `plasticity_updates_applied` beside `plasticity_updates_total`. The
| #     total counts edges visited; only applied says the arithmetic ran
| #     (D-098).
| #   - The compiled rule histogram and `plasticity_saturations_total`. Under
| #     the chain the founder's untouched allele maps to LIVE_RULE_BASE, so the
| #     standing population is about 92 percent plain Hebbian - measured. This
| #     engine's Hebbian is bounded by LEARN_LIMIT_Q16, so the clamp is the
| #     mitigation and the saturation counter measures how hard it works.
| #     Without both, an arm that destabilised because nearly everything was
| #     Hebbian would read as "learning is harmful". See ADR-0027.
| #
| # THE BASE CONFIGURATION IS THE CONFIRMATORY CAMPAIGN'S, UNCHANGED, so this
| # campaign's N arm is comparable to the published census. The only deltas are
| # the two factors. Seeds are 12 of the confirmatory campaign's 30, taken as
| # the first 12 rather than chosen, so the choice is not a degree of freedom.
| #
| # E-variable is held on in every arm (patch_capacity_scale_q16 = 262144).
| # The environment factor is not crossed here: it was crossed in the
| # confirmatory campaign and both C11.1 and C11.2 returned nulls under it, and
| # adding it would make this a 2x2x2 at 12 seeds, which has no power.
