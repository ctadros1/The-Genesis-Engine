BOTH CRITERIA ARE UNMET. BOTH ARE MEASURED NULLS WITH CONTROLS.
================================================================

  C11.1  Avar 0 of 30, Bvar 0 of 30. Bar 20, control ceiling 20. UNMET.
  C11.2  Avar 8 of 30, Bvar 0 of 30. Bar 20, control ceiling 20. UNMET.

No threshold was changed after the data was seen. The Risks table in
`planning/phase-11-lifetime-learning.md` names "plasticity is selected to
zero and the phase returns a null result" as the single most likely failure
of this phase. That is what happened, and it now has a control, a
pre-registered threshold and an effect size instead of two anecdotes.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE NUMBER THAT EXPLAINS EVERYTHING ELSE
----------------------------------------------------------------

In the Avar arm the learn phase ran **1,109,373,897 times** across 30
worlds, over **48,119 plastic edges** in 166,271 living organisms, and the
final mean absolute learned delta was **0 milli in every one of the 30
worlds** (range [0, 0]). Astat: 657,346,916 updates, 24,376 plastic edges,
again 0 milli in all 30. The B arms ran the mechanism zero times by
construction.

So the null is not "learning happened and did not change behaviour". It is
**"the mechanism executed a billion times and moved no weight by as much as
one part in a thousand"**. Every downstream number follows from that, and it
is the number a later revision should start from.

The reason is visible in the founder genotype and is a rate problem, not a
bug. `PlasticityGenes::inert()` starts every field at zero: `rule_id` 0,
all four coefficients 0, `decay` 0, `eta` 0. A plastic edge only moves its
weight when `eta` **and** the rule's coefficients are both off zero, and
point mutation reaches them one draw at a time. After 60,000 ticks at ten
times the shipped point rate, 26,654 of 684,370 edge alleles (3.9%) had
`eta` off zero at all, and the joint event needed for a nonzero update
stayed rare enough that the population mean never reached one milli.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. C11.1 - WITHIN-LIFETIME BEHAVIOURAL CHANGE. UNMET.
----------------------------------------------------------------

Decision rule, from the pre-registration: under Avar, in at least 20 of 30
worlds, the signed rank correlation between the relocation label and the
per-individual window distance must exceed the 95th percentile of its own
permutation null; under Bvar the same count must be below 20.

                       Avar     Astat    Bvar     Bstat
  worlds                 30        30       30        30
  extinct                 1         0        0         0
  boundaries used       780       780      780       780
  individuals       560,994   358,584  595,158   377,174
  matched pairs     782,755   533,155  832,481   561,377
  discarded       2,154,437 1,230,966 2,272,981 1,292,285
  median rho (milli)    -50       -58      -57       -64
  rho range        [-72, 0] [-79,-41] [-85,-19] [-87,-44]
  null p95 range    [6, 54]   [8, 13]  [7, 11]   [7, 14]
  DIRECTED count (bar)    0         0        0         0
  two-sided count        29        30       30        30
  event_wins        225,253   174,609  240,026   182,438
  control_wins      277,768   224,453  298,069   239,149
  ties              279,734   134,093  294,386   139,790
  event win share     0.448     0.438    0.446     0.433

Seed-paired Avar against Bvar on the correlation itself: mean difference
+3 milli, 95% interval [-1, +8], 14 of 30 worlds directed, p = 0.707. There
is no plasticity effect on this statistic of any size the campaign could
resolve.

**The two-sided count of 29-30 of 30 in every arm is an artefact, and the
E-stationary arm is what proves it.** The association is real, strong and
consistent - individuals' action distributions are *more* stable across a
relocation boundary than across the matched within-epoch boundary - but it
is **strongest in Bstat**, the arm with plasticity disabled *and* a
zero-magnitude schedule, where nothing whatever happens at the relocation
tick. An association that survives the removal of the event cannot have been
caused by the event. What is left is a property of where the two boundaries
sit inside the epoch, which the pre-registered design treats as matched and
which this campaign shows is not: the control boundary sits 1,000 ticks
later in each organism's life than the event boundary it is paired with.

That is a defect in the C11.1 statistic, discovered by its own environmental
control, and it is reported rather than smoothed over. It does not change
the decision - the directed count is 0 of 30 in every arm and the criterion
is unmet either way - but it means the two-sided column must not be read as
evidence of anything, and a future revision of C11.1 needs a control
boundary matched on age as well as on epoch position.

**Whether the instrument could have varied at all**, which is the check a
null of this kind is worthless without. Column occupancy over every window
used, summed across the 30 Avar worlds:

  rest              0
  move_ahead       93,611,688
  turn_left         3,845,475
  turn_right    1,468,052,837
  eat           1,565,510,000   (saturated: equals the organism-ticks)
  mate          1,565,510,000   (saturated)
  attack            0

Three of seven columns carry variation and `varying_columns` is 3 in the
median world of every arm, so the measurement was not taken against a
constant - which is what the substrate was rebuilt to guarantee. But
`turn_right` is 94% of all locomotion ticks and `rest`, `turn_left` at 0.25%
and `attack` are effectively unreachable, so the behavioural space this
criterion could observe is thin. The median per-individual distance is 0 in
both E-variable arms: most organisms hold one heading band across both
windows and contribute a tie. C11.1's null is therefore a null about a
narrow behavioural repertoire, and that limitation belongs on the result.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. C11.2 - LEARNING UNDER SELECTION. UNMET.
----------------------------------------------------------------

Decision rule: under Avar, in at least 20 of 30 worlds, either the mean
`eta` or the plastic-allele fraction must exceed the neutral marker's
matched quantity by at least 25 milli; under Bvar the same count must be
below 20.

                                    Avar   Astat    Bvar   Bstat
  worlds counted (eta or flag)         8       8       0       0
  worlds on the eta scale              0       0       0       0
  worlds on the plastic-flag scale     8       8       0       0
  drift undefined (no variance)        1       0       0       0
  median mean eta (milli)              0       0       0       0
  median marker value (milli)          0       0       1       0
  median eta excess (milli)            0       0      -1       0
  eta excess range                [-2, 1] [-2, 4] [-4, 0] [-1, 0]
  median plastic fraction (milli)     75      68       0       0
  median marker set fraction (milli)  61      61      72      68
  median plastic excess (milli)        1      -2     -76     -68
  plastic excess range        [-108, 79] [-118,138] [-203,-27] [-193,-12]
  edge alleles                   684,370 375,318 684,525 361,486
  marker alleles                 366,871 201,117 373,882 193,543
  eta alleles off founder value   26,654  15,699       0       0
  marker alleles off founder      13,751   6,975  20,967   6,580

**The drift control is live and it is what the criterion is decided
against.** In Bvar, `eta` and `EDGE_FLAG_PLASTIC` are frozen by
construction - 0 of 684,525 edge alleles moved, 0 plastic edges - while
20,967 marker alleles moved and 7.2% of marker alleles carry the neutral
flag. So the marker experiences the real population size, the real variance
in reproductive success, the real linkage and the real mutation regime, and
under those forces a neutral flag reaches roughly 7% of alleles in 60,000
ticks by drift alone. That is the empirical bar.

Against that bar, plasticity in the Avar arm reaches **the same place**: the
plastic-allele fraction is 75 milli against the marker's 61 in the median
world, an excess of +1 milli, against a 25-milli bar that is one expected
mutational step. Eight worlds clear it (excesses +29 to +79 milli), eight
worlds in the E-stationary arm clear it too, and the excess is negative in
about as many. The `eta` scale does not move at all: 0 of 30 in every arm,
median excess 0, whole-campaign range [-4, +4] milli.

**One contrast in the raw report must not be misread.** `contrast
treatment=Avar control=Bvar quantity=plastic_excess_milli` shows a mean
difference of +83 milli with a 95% interval of [62, 105] and p = 0. That is
**mechanically guaranteed by the gate and is not evidence for C11.2**: under
Bvar the plastic fraction is pinned at zero, so its "excess" is just minus
the marker's fraction. The comparison that decides C11.2 is Avar's excess
over its *own* marker, and that is +1 milli.

Reading: plasticity spread at the drift rate and not above it. It was not
selected away either - the flag is as common as its neutral twin - which is
consistent with a trait that costs almost nothing because it does almost
nothing. With `mean_abs_learned_milli` at 0 in all 60 A-arm worlds, a
plastic edge in this campaign is very nearly a neutral marker itself, and
the criterion is measuring two neutral loci against each other.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE ENVIRONMENTAL FACTOR DID BITE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Median final population: Avar 5,964, Astat 2,846, Bvar 5,653, Bstat 2,716.
The E-variable arms carry roughly twice the population of their
zero-magnitude controls at the same seeds, so the relocation schedule's
magnitude is a real ecological treatment and not a decoration. The
pre-registered calibration rule for the patch is therefore satisfied by the
confirmatory run as well as by the pilot.

One Avar world (seed 0x1786) went extinct; its allele census is empty and it
is reported as `drift_no_variance`, not as a C11.2 failure.

----------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Nothing here shows that lifetime learning is impossible in this world. It
  shows that under this genotype, this mutation regime and this 60,000-tick
  horizon, the learn phase produced no measurable learned state and the
  plastic-edge trait spread at exactly the rate a neutral locus does.
- The two-sided behavioural association is not a finding about relocation.
  Its own zero-magnitude control reproduces it.
- The eight Avar worlds that cleared the C11.2 flag bar are not eight
  positive results. Eight E-stationary worlds cleared it as well, and the
  campaign's own decision rule requires 20.
- No claim is made about cognition, about learning anything useful, or about
  behaviour changing in a way selection did not produce.

----------------------------------------------------------------
CORRECTION 1. THE CENTRAL SENTENCE OF SECTION 1 IS WRONG.
----------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT SAYS:

  "the mechanism executed a billion times and moved no weight by as much as
  one part in a thousand"

  resting on "the final mean absolute learned delta was **0 milli in every
  one of the 30 worlds** (range [0, 0])".

WHAT IS TRUE. The mean is arithmetically correct and the reading is not.
`mean_abs_learned_milli` is a mean over every plastic edge alive, and the
phenomenon is rare:

  sum |learned| in the Avar arm      139,116 Q16 = 2.1227 weight units
  rows (plastic edges)                48,119
  139,116 / 48,119 -> milli           truncates to 0

  rows with a NONZERO learned weight      25, in 14 of the 30 worlds
  largest                             15,054 Q16 = 0.2297 units = 229 milli
                                      against a clamp of 8,000 milli
  median of the 25 nonzero             2,930 Q16 = 45 milli
  Astat                                   15 rows, in 10 of 30 worlds
  Bvar, Bstat                              0, by construction

**Learning did occur**, in about 0.05 percent of the arm, far below what the
population statistic could resolve. A population where 25 edges learned and
one where none did report the same 0. This is D-098, and it is the same
shape as D-074: a summary that makes a rare signal unreadable, applied to
the one metric the phase used to answer "did anything actually learn".

`Metrics` now carries `learned_edges_nonzero` and `max_abs_learned_milli`
beside the mean (commit 3b52d98), so the next report cannot make this
reading from a zero alone.

----------------------------------------------------------------
CORRECTION 2. "1,109,373,897 UPDATES" IS 95.43 PERCENT NO-OPS.
----------------------------------------------------------------

`plasticity_updates_total` is `PlasticityCounters::total_evaluated`, which
is applied + static + refused. In the Avar arm:

  total evaluated             1,109,373,897
  applied (a rule ran)           50,661,864   4.57%
  static (rule 0, no-op)      1,058,712,033  95.43%
  refused                                 0

`StepKind::Static` is the early return at `plasticity.rs:375` for a flagged
edge whose `rule_id` is 0, taken before eta, the coefficients, the decay or
the modulator are read at all. Of the 48,119 expressed plastic edges, only
**3,147 (6.54 percent) carry a non-static rule**; 44,972 carry rule 0.

So "the learn phase ran a billion times" describes the flag, not the
mechanism. The mechanism ran 50.7 million times on 3,147 edges.

----------------------------------------------------------------
CORRECTION 3. THE EXPLANATION NAMES TWO GENES. THERE ARE FOUR
CONDITIONS, PLUS A FIFTH FOR TWO OF THE FIVE RULES.
----------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT SECTION 1 SAYS: "A plastic edge only moves its weight when `eta` **and**
the rule's coefficients are both off zero".

WHAT IS TRUE. On one edge locus, all at once:

  1 EDGE_FLAG_PLASTIC set          point-mutation target 1, a toggle
  2 rule_id != RULE_STATIC         target 2, a re-draw
  3 eta > 0                        target 3
  4 a nonzero coefficient          target 4 - and RULE_OJA is exempt from
                                   this one only (plasticity.rs:388)
  5 for rules 3 and 4, a modulator resolving to a node whose role is
    Modulatory, or world.rs:3953 hands the update a hard 0.0

Measured over the Avar arm's 684,370 edge alleles: 9 satisfy conditions 1-4
- 13 per million, in 4 of 30 worlds - against a conjunction-depth histogram
of 76.6 / 19.9 / 3.3 / 0.22 / 0.0013 percent at depths 0 to 4. Diploid
expression assembles the conjunction across haplotypes, so 59 of the 48,119
expressed plastic edges clear it.

Independence over the four marginals predicts 12.0 alleles against 9
observed: four independent rare events with no interaction. And the alleles
are not independent draws - Avar's 9 come from 4 worlds with 6 in one of
them - so **the honest count of independent assembly events is at most 4**.

**And the reason is not only rate.** Every incomplete state on the path
computes bit-identically to the founder, so selection cannot see it, while
`learn_phase` charges every flagged edge with no reference to its rule
(`world.rs:4008`) - 0.2 milli-EU per tick against a 10 milli-EU basal. The
Avar arm paid 221,410,876 milli-EU for plastic edges, about 211 million of
it for rule-0 no-ops. The interior of the path is exactly neutral and the
flagged half is deleterious: a plateau with a moat, not a gradient.

The caveat that keeps this honest: at 7.98 percent of edge alleles carrying
the flag, against the marker's neighbourhood, the cost was not resolvable
against drift in this data. It is enough to forbid a positive gradient; it
is not enough to have visibly purged the flag.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT CORRECT
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Section 2's reading of C11.1 is corrected in a different direction and
  more sharply, by D-100 rather than by the census. This file says the
  non-age-matched control makes the two-sided column unreadable but "does
  not change the decision". That is true of **this** campaign only. The
  offset alone produces rho = +158 against a null of 30 and **passes**
  `within_lifetime_shift` in a world with no event in it, with a
  dose-response of 76/158/334/700 at offsets 500/1,000/2,000/4,000. The
  sign follows the substrate's age trend, so a substrate with the opposite
  trend turns 0 of 30 into a pass. The directed statistic is not
  identified. Nothing was changed to accommodate this; the correction
  belongs to a future pre-registration (`docs/21-open-questions.md`).
- Section 3's warning that `contrast treatment=Avar control=Bvar
  quantity=plastic_excess_milli` is mechanically guaranteed by the gate is
  correct and does not go far enough: the **criterion line** has the same
  property. With `eta` and the flag frozen in the B arms, `control_count`
  is 0 by construction, so C11.2's "the control stays strictly below the
  ceiling" clause was satisfied mechanically. It did not matter, because
  the criterion failed on its treatment count alone (8 < 20). C11.2's live
  control is the within-arm neutral marker, which is matched and does move.
- Section 2's per-column occupancy numbers stand and were reproduced
  independently on fresh worlds by a from-scratch `.alac` reader (D-101):
  `eat == age` and `mate == age` in 100.0000 percent of 1,175,285 records,
  `rest == 0` and `attack == 0` in 100.0000 percent.
- The verdicts, the counts, the bar, the control ceiling and every
  threshold are untouched.

ONE NUMBER IN THIS FILE DISAGREES WITH THE REPORT IT SUMMARIZES. The
"median rho (milli)" row in section 2 gives -50 for Avar and -57 for Bvar;
`phase11-c111-confirmatory-plasticity.txt` records `med_rho_milli=-55` and
`-58`. With 30 worlds the median is between two order statistics and
`paired::median_milli` returns `sorted[(n-1)/2]`, the lower of the two;
this file took the upper. The report is what the code computed and is the
primary record. Astat (-58) and Bstat (-64) agree because their two middle
values are equal. Nothing downstream reads this row.
