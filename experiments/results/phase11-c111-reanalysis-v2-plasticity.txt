================================================================
THE VERDICTS DO NOT MOVE. BOTH CRITERIA REMAIN UNMET.
================================================================

  C11.1  Avar 0 of 30, Bvar 0 of 30. Bar 20, ceiling 20. UNMET (v1: same).
  C11.2  Avar 8 of 30, Bvar 0 of 30. Bar 20. UNMET (v1: same). C11.2 does
         not read the shift statistic, so it is unaffected by construction.

Median rho, v1 -> v2:  Avar -55 -> -46,  Astat -58 -> -56,
                       Bvar -58 -> -46,  Bstat -64 -> -61.

The exclusion of single-label strata cost about 18 percent of observations
(seed 0x1771: 8,674 dropped, 27 of 37 strata informative). NO world in any
arm was refused as `no_informative_strata` - the demographic precondition
the stratified design needs is comfortably met by real worlds, which record
tens of thousands of births against a founder cohort of a few hundred.

----------------------------------------------------------------
THE FINDING THAT MATTERS, AND IT IS NOT A GOOD ONE
----------------------------------------------------------------

**A strong negative association survives the correction in all four arms,
including Bstat.** The two-sided `associated` count is 29 to 30 of 30 in
every arm; median |rho| is 46 to 61 against nulls of 9 to 14.

Bstat has plasticity disabled AND a zero-magnitude relocation schedule.
Nothing happens at its event tick. An association that survives removing
the event was not caused by the event - which is the same argument that
exposed D-100 in the first place, now applying to what replaced it.

So: the age offset is fixed and provably fixed. On the synthetic tripwire,
where the age offset is the only difference and no event exists, v2 reads
0 against a null of 6 where v1 read +158 against 30 and passed, with the
dose-response flat at zero across gaps of 500 to 4,000 and across 240 to
7,680 pairs. **That artifact is gone. A different one is still here.**

What the numbers point at, stated as a lead and not as a conclusion:
`event_wins` is consistently below `control_wins` (seed 0x1771: 6,342
against 7,231), and in the E-stationary arms the medians separate outright
(Astat event 8, control 52; Bstat event 16, control 64). Behaviour is
systematically MORE stable across a relocation boundary than across a
mid-epoch boundary, in arms where the relocation does nothing. Age is now
matched, so the remaining difference between the two pairs is their
position in the epoch and their absolute tick.

**Consequence for C11.1.** The directed decision is unaffected: the rule
requires a POSITIVE correlation and this association is negative, so it
refuses in every arm either way. The 0-of-30 verdict is sound. But the
statistic is still not identified, and a future positive result from it
could not be trusted until this second confound is explained. C11.1 should
not be re-run for a positive claim on the strength of this fix alone.
