READING, AGAINST THE LOCKED DECISION RULES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. C16.5 MET. `scratch` runs end to end: from chemistry, through
   abiogenesis and the microbial field, to materialized individual
   organisms, in 30 of 30 worlds under N and under both scaffold
   conditions, against the pre-registered bar of 28 of 30 - and in 0 of
   30 under the transition-disabled control T0, by construction. The
   T0-versus-N difference is the finding; the first materialization
   lands between ticks 22,500 and 24,500 (medians), inside the 20,000-
   30,000 window the pre-registration stated. The scaffold contrast on
   materialization is flat at the ceiling, as stated in advance
   (D-128's field is rich everywhere); the scaffolds bring the first
   materialization forward by 1,000-2,000 ticks (a small, consistent,
   un-barred difference, reported as the curve).

2. C16.6: THE PRE-REGISTERED EXPECTED NULL. No lineage exceeded one
   module in any of the 120 worlds (peak max_modules = 1 wherever
   organisms existed; multi-module fraction 0). This is a measured
   result about reachability at these rates and this horizon, reported
   as such and not weakened after the fact. Which of the two
   pre-registered reasons applies: births DO occur - in 20 of 30 N worlds
   (median 8.5 per world) and 25 of 30 scaffold worlds (median ~76) -
   against ~22,800-23,300 materialized organisms per world, so a
   materialized lineage reproduces on the order of once per 300-2,700
   arrivals. Structural evolution had almost no material to act on
   inside the horizon: the null is a birth-limit null (D-073's shape), as
   the pre-registration said it would be if births stayed rare.

3. THE INFLUX RATE IS THE CAP, AS STATED IN ADVANCE. Every transition
   world's materialized total is ~22,800-23,300 at the horizon, which is
   the per-tick cap (64 per 100-tick check) applied from first onset -
   the trigger fires in far more slots than the cap admits, every check.
   The standing population (medians 150 / 185 / 184) is therefore the
   equilibrium of a configured influx against starvation, and the
   pre-registration forbids reading it as a property of the field.

4. UNPLANNED OBSERVATION (no bar, no decision): the scaffolds raise
   births roughly ninefold (medians 76 and 75.5 against 8.5) and the
   standing population by ~23%, with the same influx. A plausible
   mechanism - patch cells hold more substrate, so more slots trigger
   near each other and materialized unicells land beside potential mates
   more often - is consistent with the numbers but was not instrumented;
   it is a hypothesis for Phase 19's pre-registration, not a finding.

NAMED NARROWINGS (carried from ADR-0031 and ADR-0032)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

  materialized unicell (a slow, blind gut) starves in ~200 ticks against
  a trait-derived maturity of 800 unless it finds biomass; C16.2's
  survival clause leans on this narrowness (ADR-0032), and Phase 19
  (chemistry as food) is the named follow-on that re-asks C16.6 under a
  viable unicell ecology.
- The class-to-genome map is constant across classes (ADR-0032); the
  registry decides which slots trigger, not what arrives.
