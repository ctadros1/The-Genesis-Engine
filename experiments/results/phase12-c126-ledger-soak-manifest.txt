| # Phase 12 confirmatory campaign: C12.1, C12.2 and C12.3.
| #
| # ============================================================
| # PRE-REGISTRATION. Committed before the campaign was run.
| # The companion document is experiments/phase12-artifact-preregistration.md;
| # the decision rules and every constant they use are in
| # sim_analysis::ArtifactPlan::preregistered and are echoed into the report.
| # ============================================================
| #
| # WHAT THIS CAMPAIGN IS FOR. C12.1, C12.2 and C12.3 have stood at NOT
| # BUILT since the phase opened. The artifact half now exists (ADR-0028,
| # commits 80e73d3..07d773b) and this campaign gives the three criteria their
| # controls and their thresholds. A null on any of them is a result and is
| # reported as one; the plan's own risk table names C12.3 as the criterion
| # most likely to return null and says so in advance.
| #
| # THE PHASE'S OWN PRIOR IS UNFAVOURABLE AND IS RECORDED HERE RATHER THAN
| # DISCOVERED LATER. Two pilots on disjoint seeds (39321 and 30583, on the
| # phase2 default world, 60,000 ticks, binding 0.25) showed: strikes in the
| # thousands, pick-ups in the tens, placements zero, combinations zero in a
| # thriving world of 2,400; and a collapsed world of 7 with nothing. Every
| # object action needs a lineage to have bound its channel by mutation
| # (D-114) and, for placement and combination, to have bound two channels
| # in one line of descent - the same conjunction structure that produced
| # Phase 11's reachability null. That is the structure of the question, not
| # a reason to change it.
| #
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| # CONDITIONS. Four arms, 30 seeds each, matched on seed and horizon.
| # ------------------------------------------------------------
| #
| #   A  artifacts persistent, combination enabled     (artifact defaults)
| #   B  artifacts ephemeral: what lands is destroyed at the end of its tick
| #      (artifact.ephemeral true); actions exist and cost the same
| #   C  actions permitted but physically inert (artifact.inert true): every
| #      requested action is charged and counted, nothing changes; the control
| #      that separates "actions fire" from "actions pay"
| #   D  combination disabled (artifact.max_composition_depth 0)
| #
| # Every arm carries the same bind rate, so the mutational supply of object
| # bindings is common-mode; every arm carries the contest section, so
| # carcass objects exist in all four (they are ecology, not action).
| #
| # THE BASE WORLD is the Phase 11 confirmatory base (128 x 128, 200
| # founders, capacity 240,000, physiology with the extrinsic hazard at 13,
| # point rate 65535, duplication 6554) with the relocating patch OFF: the
| # yield layer needs the worldmod section, the schedule does not belong in a
| # question about objects. Chosen because it is a known-stable ecology with
| # births in the hundreds per 10,000 ticks, not for anything about objects.
| #
| # BINDING RATE 16384 (0.25 per birth). Calibrated on the pilots above by a
| # rule about the mechanism, not the outcome: at 0.10 the thriving pilot
| # reached its first pick-up at tick ~40,000 with 4 in 60,000; at 0.25 it
| # reached 23. The rule was "the median pilot world binds and successfully
| # fires pick_up inside the horizon"; 0.25 satisfies it in the thriving
| # pilot and nothing satisfies it in the collapsed one. Higher rates were not
| # tried, so this is a lower bound on what the campaign could have used, and
| # it is recorded as such.
| #
| # EFFECT SIZES AND BARS (sim_analysis::ArtifactPlan::preregistered):
| #   C12.1  successful pick_up + place + combine per million organism-ticks,
| #          A minus C, SESOI 10 ppm, directed increase, bar 20 of 30
| #   C12.2a median placed-object lifetime > median organism lifespan, 15 of 30
| #   C12.2b stratified exposure effect on offspring per thousand ticks of
| #          life > 0, exposure floor 5% of life, at least 20 exposed
| #          organisms per world, bar 20 of 30; both halves required
| #   C12.3  depth-two composites per thousand organisms, last third minus
| #          first third of the run, SESOI 0.5 per thousand (500 milli),
| #          N = 20 of 30 - the N the plan left unstated, fixed here as the
| #          smallest count with a one-sided binomial p < 0.05 at a null rate
| #          of one half, the same bar C12.1 states; D asserted zero
| #   Extinct worlds count against every bar. Analysis seed 0xa11fac750b1ec751.
| #
| # EVENT LOGS ON: the analysis reads them. Actions off, spatial off.
